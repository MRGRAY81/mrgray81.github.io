About me

Curiosity backed by a genuine desire to help.

I recognised the transformative potential of the internet before the dot-com boom. Today, I see similarly far-reaching possibilities in AI — particularly when it helps more people create, learn and participate.

Empathy, honesty and adaptability have shaped my approach throughout my life. Long before working in adult support services, I enjoyed understanding people's challenges, communicating clearly and helping them recognise what they could achieve.

I believe technology should expand people's opportunities, not make them feel excluded. My aim is to make complex ideas easier to understand and useful tools easier to access.
